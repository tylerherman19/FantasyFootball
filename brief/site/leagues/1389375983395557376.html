How leans work

Each starter gets a deterministic lean. We compare the player's team efficiency in the position-relevant phase (EPA per dropback for QBs/WRs/TEs, EPA per rush for RBs) against the league median, and add how much EPA the opponent's defense allows in that phase relative to median. A combined edge above +0.03 EPA/play is a Start; below −0.03 is a Sit; anything between is Neutral. Team defenses use the inverse (low EPA allowed is good). Write-ups only use stats actually present in the data — nothing is estimated.
